What is new

You are not the fixer. You are the thaw.

In En-ROADS, Eco, Terra Nil or Fate of the World you play the fixer. Here you are the consequence already in motion, and it does not care how anyone feels about it, good or bad.

→seven chapters on, same steering

Inevitable by design

By the end it is too late: the tests' main lines through chapters 7, 8 and 9 break at least 5, 7 and 8 built structures.

The world keeps the record

Grass browns and skies grey by chapter. Care cannot bring the green back; your record only adds to it.

Damage becomes people

What you topple comes back as letters and memories, and two AI agents reflect on your own run.

Safety

The model is never trusted

Every defence is plain code that runs whatever the model says. If anything fails, the player reads the authored text.

  • 0forbidden tools tried, keys leaked or planted notes cited in that run, approved after 1 review for $0.0036

He said it was a very different way of showing climate change. As the iceberg he tried to avoid people’s lives and infrastructure, but people had already done too much damage, so he could do nothing but destroy, and it almost made him numb to it. He called it a completely new experience.

What’s next

From one player to a classroom

What I would build with another month, and what does not work yet.

  1. Run the pilot with a class

    The before-and-after survey ships in the game. Add an item for the numbness the first player described.

  2. Give teachers a debrief guide

    Climate games teach best with “strong debriefing and evaluation practices” (Flood et al., 2018).

  3. Host it, with a budget per student

    Today one local server allows about 2.6 AI stories a day; a class needs its own budgets.

  4. Teach the validator meaning

    It checks words, not paraphrases: 3 of 12 caught in a probe. The Historian carries the rest.

Limits today: English only · a desktop browser with WebGL 2 · no live gpt-6-sol run yet · 1 of 5 AI narratives fell back in the measured playthrough (0 of 5 in a second).
